Technical specifications, system documentation, and development architecture records

PROJECT
ANGUARD
Project VanGuard is an automated, privacy-preserving traffic analytics system developed for Digos City. It uses an AI-powered edge device to detect helmetless motorcycle riders in real time.

Edge Computing Unit

Handles all detection and analysis directly on the device itself.

Optical Sensor

A weather-resistant, high-definition camera designed to capture clear traffic feeds across designated lanes.

Data Sync

Violation records including count, time, and location are automatically saved and transmitted to the central monitoring dashboard.

MISSION

Our goal is to combine embedded systems and computer vision to make the roadways of Digos City safer. By providing reliable compliance tracking and trend forecasting, this platform helps optimize traffic deployment and encourages responsible riding habits.

VISION

We envision a smarter, data-driven traffic culture where modern technology seamlessly protects every motorist, significantly reducing road accidents and inspiring safer riding habits across the region.
